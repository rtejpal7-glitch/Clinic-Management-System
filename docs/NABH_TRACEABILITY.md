# NABH requirement traceability — ClinicFlow PoC

**Primary reference:** NABH Digital Health Standards for Clinic Management Systems (CMS), 1st Edition, September 2025 (effective 17 Sep 2025).
**Scope of this matrix:** the CPD (Clinics, Polyclinics & Dispensary) objective elements that the PoC targets: AAC.1, AAC.2, COP.1, FPM.3, DAC.1, DAC.2, plus the supporting elements DOM.3, IMS.1a, IMS.1d and IMS.1f. **Version 0.2 (dermatology extension)** adds COP.5a, COP.8a, MOM.2a–c, MOM.3a, IMS.2b and DOM.1a, and updates COP.1a, COP.1d, COP.1g, DAC.2a–c (see "Dermatology extension" below).

The element numbering, category (Core / Commitment / Achievement / Excellence) and head (CPD / LTS) below were checked line by line against the source PDF. Requirement summaries are paraphrased, not quoted. The NABH Digital Health Standards for **Hospitals** (2nd Ed.) were **not** used for this matrix, and their numbering is not mixed in.

> **Important.** This matrix records what the proof of concept does. It is **not** a compliance claim. ClinicFlow is not NABH-certified or ABDM-certified. Under the CMS standard, a product must first be evaluated and approved by NHA for ABDM and security requirements before it can apply for NABH certification. The standard also has elements that need external certification (for example AAC.1f, IMS.1a, ISO 27001 under IMS.3a, and WASA under IMS.3b). Certification at Basic level needs 100% of Core, 60% of Commitment and 30% of Achievement elements in the selected scope.

**Status legend:** Not Started · In Progress (partially met; see the gap) · Implemented (built, no automated test) · Tested (built and covered by an automated test) · Not Applicable

Test references: `api:` = `tests/api/<file>::<test>`, `unit:` = `backend/test/unit_tests.cpp`, `e2e:` = `frontend/e2e/run-e2e.mjs`, `e2e-derm:` = `frontend/e2e/run-derm-e2e.mjs`, `fe:` = `frontend/tests/validation.test.ts`.

**Related obligations outside the CMS standard (not NABH elements):**
- *Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:* network sharing requires a recorded data-sharing agreement reference at clinic level; patients can opt out (and contributed records are withdrawn); network records hold no direct identifiers and use keyed pseudonyms, month-level dates and coarse age bands; small cohorts are suppressed. Whether this meets the Act and its rules for a given deployment is a legal assessment the clinic must make; ClinicFlow does not provide the agreement, privacy notice or legal basis.
- *ABDM / FHIR:* unchanged from v0.1 (mock gateway). Dermatology findings and photographs are not yet mapped to FHIR (Observation/Media) — see ROADMAP.
- *Clinical photographs:* captured only with recorded consent and scope; stored encrypted; views audited; never deleted (entered-in-error instead).
