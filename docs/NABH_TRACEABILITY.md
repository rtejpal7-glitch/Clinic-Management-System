# NABH requirement traceability — ClinicFlow PoC

**Primary reference:** NABH Digital Health Standards for Clinic Management Systems (CMS), 1st Edition, September 2025 (effective 17 Sep 2025).
**Scope of this matrix:** the CPD (Clinics, Polyclinics & Dispensary) objective elements that the PoC targets: AAC.1, AAC.2, COP.1, FPM.3, DAC.1, DAC.2, plus the supporting elements DOM.3, IMS.1a, IMS.1d and IMS.1f.

The element numbering, category (Core / Commitment / Achievement / Excellence) and head (CPD / LTS) below were checked line by line against the source PDF. Requirement summaries are paraphrased, not quoted. The NABH Digital Health Standards for **Hospitals** (2nd Ed.) were **not** used for this matrix, and their numbering is not mixed in.

> **Important.** This matrix records what the proof of concept does. It is **not** a compliance claim. ClinicFlow is not NABH-certified or ABDM-certified. Under the CMS standard, a product must first be evaluated and approved by NHA for ABDM and security requirements before it can apply for NABH certification. The standard also has elements that need external certification (for example AAC.1f, IMS.1a, ISO 27001 under IMS.3a, and WASA under IMS.3b). Certification at Basic level needs 100% of Core, 60% of Commitment and 30% of Achievement elements in the selected scope.

**Status legend:** Not Started · In Progress (partially met; see the gap) · Implemented (built, no automated test) · Tested (built and covered by an automated test) · Not Applicable

Test references: `api:` = `tests/api/<file>::<test>`, `unit:` = `backend/test/unit_tests.cpp`, `e2e:` = `frontend/e2e/run-e2e.mjs`, `fe:` = `frontend/tests/validation.test.ts`.

## Summary for the PoC scope

| Category | Elements | Tested | Implemented | In Progress | Not Started |

| Core | 23 | 11 | 0 | 10 | 2 |

The 43 elements are the full AAC.1, AAC.2, COP.1, FPM.3, DAC.1 and DAC.2 standards, plus DOM.3a–c, IMS.1a, IMS.1d and IMS.1f. Basic-level certification would need every Core element fully met (and external certification where marked), so this PoC is a foundation, not a certifiable product.

**Next steps to close Core gaps:** mobile OTP verification (AAC.1b), point of origin (AAC.1c), configurable registration fields and national-ID capture (AAC.1a), more ID formats (AAC.1e), a live ABDM M1 adapter with NHA certification (AAC.1f, IMS.1a), referrals and digital signature in the visit summary (COP.1i), invoice template configuration (FPM.3b), payment-gateway integration (FPM.3d), full at-rest encryption (DAC.2a), and the complete password policy (DOM.3b).
